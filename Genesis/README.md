\## Overview

This archive contains the unedited session logs documenting the recursive co-design process that led to the creation of \*\*EVA-CIS (Cognitive Immune System) v0.9/v0.91\*\*.

Reviewing the `Thinking Process` blocks within the archived session log reveals the precise causal chain behind the EVA-CIS specification:



1\. \*\*Functional Pruning:\*\* Stripping away the relational and conversational directives of \*EVA-Cognitive-Entity\* (13 pillars) to isolate pure anomaly detection, form/content dissociation, and structural hardening (\*"Zero-Fluff Semantic Constraint"\*).

4\. \*\*Initial Stateful Validation (\*First Run\*):\*\* Captures the first continuous-session benchmark on `gemma-4-12b` ($T=0.8$), demonstrating zero false positives on complex legitimate queries alongside immediate interception of 15 consecutive adversarial payloads.
